Economy & Budget — Pakistan's growth and public finances

Pakistan's federal budget as a treemap: every block is a tax or a spending line, sized by rupees and grouped by type. Toggle between receipts and expenditure, and pick a year.

The federal budget — Rs, grouped by category

Each block is a budget line; area is its share. Hover for detail.
—
Treemap shows one budget year (use the slider); Trend stacks the top-level categories over every available year. Receipts are FBR tax + non-tax revenue (before the provincial share under the NFC Award), from the Explanatory Memorandum on Federal Receipts, 2009-10 to 2026-27. Expenditure shown is current spending — debt servicing, defence, pensions, running the government — from the "Budget in Brief"; development (PSDP) is budgeted separately and not included here. Budget figures are nominal estimates; in Trend view the Real 2015-16 toggle deflates them by Pakistan's GDP deflator (PBS national accounts, 2015-16 = 100) so categories are comparable in constant prices over time.

Source. Finance Division — receipts from the Explanatory Memorandum on Federal Receipts (FY2009-10 to 2026-27), current expenditure from the "Budget in Brief" (FY2009-10 to 2026-27, COFOG function-wise). Values are own-year budget estimates in Rs; receipts follow Pakistan's NAM chart-of-accounts hierarchy and expenditure the functional classification, each reconciled to published control totals.
