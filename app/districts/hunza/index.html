Hunza district

Hunza district, Gilgit-Baltistan, lies outside the tables PBS has published from Census 2023, so this profile draws on surveys and satellite sources. The median resident is 309 minutes (about 5.2 hours) on foot from the nearest health facility. In 2025 the mean temperature was -12.3°C, 1.41°C above the 1981–2010 normal, with 0 days at 40°C or more.

How to read this profile

  • PBS has not published Census 2023 district tables for Gilgit-Baltistan or Azad Jammu and Kashmir, so the census rows are absent and the surveys that reach here are shown instead.
  • Rank 1 is the highest value among the districts that have a figure, whichever way is better. The province and Pakistan columns are the median district, not a population-weighted total.
  • Survey estimates (PSLM, MICS, DHS, LFS, HIES) are indicative at district level: the surveys were designed for provinces, and Data Darbar post-stratifies and suppresses small samples.
  • Rows marked ‡ are one figure the source published for the district before it was divided, shown for each of its successors; it counts once in the ranks and medians.
  • Climate figures are district means of ERA5-Land and CHIRPS grids; the normal is 1981–2010. Travel times are from the Malaria Atlas accessibility surfaces weighted by where people live.
  • Hunza Nagar is now Hunza, Nagar. The figure is for the whole of the old district and is shown across all of them, not divided between them
  • This district's figure also covers NAGAR DISTRICT, which the emigration register does not list separately
