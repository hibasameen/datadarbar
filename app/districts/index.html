About these profiles

The profiles are generated from the same tables as the Places map and the catalogue, on the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics’ 2023 district frame. Census 2023 does not publish district tables for Gilgit-Baltistan or Azad Jammu and Kashmir, so their pages carry the surveys and satellite sources that reach them. Each page has a CSV of its figures; the full tables are in the place_indicators dataset. Methods.
