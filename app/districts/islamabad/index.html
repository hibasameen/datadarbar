District profile · Islamabad Capital Territory

Islamabad district

Islamabad district, Islamabad Capital Territory, had 2,363,863 people at the 2023 census, growing 2.80% a year since 2017, the 34th most populous of the 125 districts with a 2023 figure. 47% of people live in urban areas and 84.0% of those aged 10 and over can read and write. 6.0% of people were multidimensionally poor in 2019-20. The median resident is 14 minutes on foot from the nearest health facility. In 2025 the mean temperature was 21.2°C, 1.43°C above the 1981–2010 normal, with 13 days at 40°C or more.

How to read this profile

  • Rank 1 is the highest value among the districts that have a figure, whichever way is better. The province and Pakistan columns are the median district, not a population-weighted total.
  • Survey estimates (PSLM, MICS, DHS, LFS, HIES) are indicative at district level: the surveys were designed for provinces, and Data Darbar post-stratifies and suppresses small samples.
  • Climate figures are district means of ERA5-Land and CHIRPS grids; the normal is 1981–2010. Travel times are from the Malaria Atlas accessibility surfaces weighted by where people live.
