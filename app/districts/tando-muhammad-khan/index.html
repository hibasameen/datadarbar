District profile · Sindh

Tando Muhammad Khan district

Tando Muhammad Khan district, Sindh, had 726,119 people at the 2023 census, growing 1.18% a year since 2017, the 96th most populous of the 130 districts with a 2023 figure. 22% of people live in urban areas and 34.0% of those aged 10 and over can read and write. 72.3% of people were multidimensionally poor in 2019-20. The median resident is 377 minutes (about 6.3 hours) on foot from the nearest health facility. In 2025 the mean temperature was 27.0°C, 0.25°C above the 1981–2010 normal, with 49 days at 40°C or more.

    How to read this profile

    • Rank 1 is the highest value among the districts that have a figure, whichever way is better. The province and Pakistan columns are the median district, not a population-weighted total.
    • Survey estimates (PSLM, MICS, DHS, LFS, HIES) are indicative at district level: the surveys were designed for provinces, and Data Darbar post-stratifies and suppresses small samples.
    • Climate figures are district means of ERA5-Land and CHIRPS grids; the normal is 1981–2010. Travel times are from the Malaria Atlas accessibility surfaces weighted by where people live.
    • 1998 Badin + Hyderabad covers today’s Badin, Hyderabad, Matiari, Tando Allahyar, Tando Muhammad Khan; their figures are combined here
