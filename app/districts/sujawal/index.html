District profile · Sindh

Sujawal district

Sujawal district, Sindh, had 839,292 people at the 2023 census, growing 1.25% a year since 2017, the 90th most populous of the 130 districts with a 2023 figure. 11% of people live in urban areas and 27.0% of those aged 10 and over can read and write. 79.5% of people were multidimensionally poor in 2019-20. The median resident is 367 minutes (about 6.1 hours) on foot from the nearest health facility. In 2025 the mean temperature was 26.8°C, 0.17°C above the 1981–2010 normal, with 1 days at 40°C or more.

    How to read this profile

    • Rank 1 is the highest value among the districts that have a figure, whichever way is better. The province and Pakistan columns are the median district, not a population-weighted total.
    • Survey estimates (PSLM, MICS, DHS, LFS, HIES) are indicative at district level: the surveys were designed for provinces, and Data Darbar post-stratifies and suppresses small samples.
    • Climate figures are district means of ERA5-Land and CHIRPS grids; the normal is 1981–2010. Travel times are from the Malaria Atlas accessibility surfaces weighted by where people live.
    • 1998 Thatta covers today’s Sujawal, Thatta; their figures are combined here
    • Sajawal is Sujawal in PBS’s 2023 district list
