How to read this profile

  • Rank 1 is the highest value among the districts that have a figure, whichever way is better. The province and Pakistan columns are the median district, not a population-weighted total.
  • Survey estimates (PSLM, MICS, DHS, LFS, HIES) are indicative at district level: the surveys were designed for provinces, and Data Darbar post-stratifies and suppresses small samples.
  • Climate figures are district means of ERA5-Land and CHIRPS grids; the normal is 1981–2010. Travel times are from the Malaria Atlas accessibility surfaces weighted by where people live.
