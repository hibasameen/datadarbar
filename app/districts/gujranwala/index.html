How to read this profile

These figures describe the district, not necessarily the city of the same name. Literacy covers ages 10 and over; out-of-school counts cover ages 5–16. Missing values are unavailable, not zero. This profile uses 2023 figures only: boundary changes make some 2017 comparisons unsafe. School attendance and literacy are different measures.

Source and method

Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Population and Housing Census 2023, Tables 1 and 12; processed by Data Darbar. The table and CSV are generated from the same district record used by the explorer.
