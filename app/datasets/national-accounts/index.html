Definitions and limitations

Units vary BY TABLE: levels are Rs million (tables 2–5, 8–11), growth rates and shares are percentages (tables 6, 7a/b). Always read table_name before aggregating. Item labels on the Macro/Main-Aggregate sheets are best-effort.
