District boundary changes, Census 2017 to 2023

Every district-level relationship between Census 2017 and Census 2023, as groups that cover the same ground.

Read before using

A group is the smallest set of 2017 and 2023 units covering the same territory, so a merge has two 2017 units and one 2023 unit and a split has one and several. What makes this checkable rather than argued is the balances column: Census 2023 table 1 prints PBS’s own restatement of the 2017 population on 2023 boundaries, and for every group the 2017 units’ published population equals the 2023 units’ restated figure. All 127 groups balance. A group that did not would mean the relation asserted for it is wrong.
