Oil and gas royalties transferred to provinces

Royalty on crude oil and on natural gas, gas development surcharge and excise duty on gas paid to each province, FY2008-09 to FY2026-27, budget and revised estimates.

Read before using

Every estimate as printed. For one figure a year, take the revised estimate from the following year’s budget, as Adaad’s best estimate does. Before FY2011-12 the straight-transfer total includes provincial GST; use the four oil and gas items for a consistent series. Sindh receives about half, Punjab under a tenth.
