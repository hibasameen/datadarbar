Boundaries, Census 2023

The PBS Digital Census 2023 polygons, exactly as the Places map draws them: 156 districts and 649 tehsils. Districts carry PBS's own district_code; tehsils carry the census unit id dds_id, the only sub-district key with one polygon per census unit. Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are included and flagged where the census does not reach them.
