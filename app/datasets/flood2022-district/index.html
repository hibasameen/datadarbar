Read before using

From UNOSAT’s seven-day NOAA-20 composites, 11 windows on one frame. The windows are not consecutive: nothing was observed 22–25 September, 18 October–13 November or 21 November–24 December 2022, so say “in every observed window”. Satellite detection under cloud cover is incomplete; low-coverage weeks are counted. Preliminary analyses, not field-validated. The rows are COD’s 2022 districts, not the 2023 frame: Keamari has no row (its area is in Karachi South and West), and Lehri (PK218), since split between Kachhi and Sibi, has a row but no district_code.
