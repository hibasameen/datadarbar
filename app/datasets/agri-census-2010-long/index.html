Read before using

The 6th Agricultural Census (fieldwork December 2009 to June 2010) was a sample: PBS listed every household in the selected mouzas and urban blocks, enumerated clusters of them and all large holdings, and raised the result to the frame. These figures are read from the text layer of PBS’s province tabulation PDFs, not from a data file. Punjab, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are complete, in acres: 37 Punjab districts including Islamabad, the De-excluded Areas of D.G. Khan and Rajanpur, and Cholistan; 23 Sindh districts, with Karachi as one; 24 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts, the Malakand, Bajaur, Khyber and Mohmand agencies and six Frontier Regions. Balochistan’s districts are missing: the Balochistan link on PBS’s page serves the Sindh file, so the province appears only as a total. The geography is the 2010 district frame under PBS’s own unit names and spellings (JACCOBABAD, SHANGLAPAR); nothing is keyed to the 2023 districts, so do not join it to agri_census_2024_district by name without checking for the districts created or divided since. One row per printed figure. The tables run from farms and tenure (Chapter 1) through fragmentation, land use, irrigation, intensities, crops, inputs, households and labour (8), credit, machinery, livestock and housing to land ownership (13). Table 8.1 counts all households by type and Table 13.1 counts owners by area owned, which the 2024 census does not publish. col_index is the column number PBS prints under each header, not a header: read it with agri_census_2010_tables, and check the PDF page before relying on a column under a spanning header. The source file ‘PAKISTAN (hectares file)’ is in hectares and repeats the provincial totals, so sum within one source_file. flag marks a dash (nil, value 0), an asterisk or plus (lt_half, no value) and X (na). qc marks 72 rows with a blank cell, whose column numbers are positional guesses (count_mismatch), and one row split across a page whose unit is taken from the row above (unit_carried). Across all tables 96.5 per cent of province figures of 1,000 or more equal the sum of their districts; the rest are tables printed in hundreds or thousands, blank cells and source typos, and a few tables not yet traced (Sindh 8.5 and 6.20, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 11.4, Punjab and Sindh 12.1). table_id is text (6.10 is not 6.1).
