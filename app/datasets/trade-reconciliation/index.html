Read before using

Read this before quoting a trade total. Monthly country and group rows match the totals endpoint to within 0.01 US dollars billion in every month except April 2021. Quarterly rows overshoot on exports, most often in Q4 — 3.3 per cent on average — so a year built from quarters is close but not exact.
