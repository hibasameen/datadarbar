Read before using

Detections, not fires or emissions: one burning field can give several. Nominal and high confidence in n_detections, all in n_detections_all. Global Forest Watch’s copy misses 82 days, the whole of 27 July–23 August 2022 among them; days_missing_in_feed says how many fall in each month, so treat those months, and 2024–25, as floors.
