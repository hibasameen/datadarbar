District tree-cover loss by year since 2001

Hectares of tree cover lost by district and year, 2001–2025, at 10 and 30 per cent canopy, with the driver of loss and the share associated with fire.

Read before using

Tree cover is 1 per cent of Pakistan’s land, so these are small numbers: under 200 hectares a year nationally since 2013 at 30 per cent canopy. Use one threshold at a time; never add them. Loss is not net change: gain is separate and tree cover 2010 is a different product.
