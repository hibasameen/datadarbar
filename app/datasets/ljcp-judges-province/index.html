Judges in post by province

Judicial posts sanctioned, filled and vacant in the district judiciary, by province and session division, 2020 to 2024.

Read before using

Consolidated strength, all ranks together, as each report prints it. Not every year is there: no 2021 edition table, KP 2024 not found, and Islamabad prints working judges only. Punjab's 2022 edition is dated 31 December 2021 (strength_date_status). Balochistan's figures sum four rank tables and, from 2022, exclude ex-cadre posts, so its 2020 total is not strictly comparable.
