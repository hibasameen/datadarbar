Crop-burning season fires by district

VIIRS detections in the rice-residue season (October–November) and the wheat season (April–May), by district and year.

Read before using

Punjab is 70–89 per cent of October–November detections. The incomplete flags mark seasons with days missing from the feed; those are floors.
