Read before using

Punjab coordinates updated on 30 September 2026: 37,917 of 38,149 current roster schools link by inferred EMIS convention to the 2017 public-school point layer linked by Pakistan Institute of Education. These are historical source points, not verified current GPS observations. Current school names, gender, level and roster membership are retained. Read coord_source_year, coord_validation and coord_review_flags. Shared points, name differences, gender conflicts and district-boundary conflicts are flagged. The 232 unmatched current schools retain their previous positions or missing coordinates. Historical schools absent from the current roster are not added. READ coord_method AND coord_precision before using lat/lng: other regions and unmatched Punjab rows still include settlement, cluster, markaz and tehsil approximations. coord_tier retains the original regional grade. in_analysis, analysis_sex and analysis_note describe the frozen September 2026 analysis; analysis_lat, analysis_lng, analysis_district_key_boundary and the other analysis_* provenance fields preserve its positions. The companion distance, coverage and validation tables remain September snapshots and have not been recomputed from this coordinate update. gender is the name designation; in Sindh use gender_official and sex-specific enrolment for mixed-school definitions. district_key is the source district on the 147-district frame; district_key_boundary is recomputed for updated points. Building-level accuracy and current operation are not established. Compilation CC BY 4.0; underlying source rights remain with their publishers. The ArcGIS item supplies no explicit licence.
