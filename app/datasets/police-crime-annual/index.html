Definitions and limitations

Nine reporting regions, and the geography is not uniform between them: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa reports 38 places and Azad Jammu & Kashmir 11, while Punjab, Sindh, Balochistan, ICT, Gilgit-Baltistan and the Railways police report one figure each. So a district map of this covers KP and AJK and nothing else. measure says what is counted — mostly reported_offence_count, which is offences reported to police and not crimes committed.
