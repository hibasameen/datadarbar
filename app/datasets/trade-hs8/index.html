Definitions and limitations

Values are THOUSAND rupees. fy_* are full fiscal-year (Jul–Jun) cumulative figures; month_* are June alone. country IS NULL marks the commodity total row — country rows sum to it, so filter one or the other or you will double-count. Years missing here exist only as PDFs upstream (see file_catalog).
