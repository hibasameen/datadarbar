Distance-to-school distributions by sex and level

Distance-to-school distributions by region and district, sex and school level (primary, middle-plus, high-plus).

Read before using

One row per geography × sex × level. geography_type = 'region' rows are the seven analysis regions; 'district' rows carry district_key. mean_km and median_km are population-weighted over 1 km cells; share_over_2km/5km/10km are population shares beyond that straight-line distance. network_schools is the size of the REGION's network for that sex and level — the same number repeats on every district row of a region, because a district's residents can use the next district's schools. Per-district school counts are in school_access_district. tier and sector repeat the coverage ledger's grade and the 'government schools only' scope.
