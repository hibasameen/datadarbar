Read before using

133 plants. Hydel is the largest block at 11,890 MW, then coal at 7,260, furnace oil at 5,440 and nuclear at 3,635; wind has the most plants, 37, for 1,885 MW. Installed capacity is nameplate and not what a plant actually generates — see nepra_disco_annual for what was dispatched.
