District tree cover in 2000 and 2010

Tree cover in 2000 and 2010, gain 2000–2020 and loss 2001–2025, by district, at 10 and 30 per cent canopy.

Read before using

With Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir, 981,653 ha at 30 per cent canopy in 2000, 1.12 per cent of land. Tree cover 2010 is a separate product, not 2000 minus loss.
