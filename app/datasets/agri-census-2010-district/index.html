Read before using

The 6th Agricultural Census (fieldwork December 2009 to June 2010) was a sample: PBS listed every household in the selected mouzas and urban blocks, enumerated clusters of them and all large holdings, and raised the result to the frame. These figures are read from the text layer of PBS’s province tabulation PDFs, not from a data file. Punjab, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa are complete, in acres: 37 Punjab districts including Islamabad, the De-excluded Areas of D.G. Khan and Rajanpur, and Cholistan; 23 Sindh districts, with Karachi as one; 24 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts, the Malakand, Bajaur, Khyber and Mohmand agencies and six Frontier Regions. Balochistan’s districts are missing: the Balochistan link on PBS’s page serves the Sindh file, so the province appears only as a total. The geography is the 2010 district frame under PBS’s own unit names and spellings (JACCOBABAD, SHANGLAPAR); nothing is keyed to the 2023 districts, so do not join it to agri_census_2024_district by name without checking for the districts created or divided since. This table is Tables 1.1, 1.2 and 6.1 brought to one row per unit; every count sums exactly from districts to province, and the provinces with Balochistan to Pakistan’s 8,264,480 farms. Shares are in per cent.

Provenance

Includes derived

The publisher’s figures, with columns Data Darbar constructed.

Counts and areas are PBS’s; the average farm size, the shares and the cropping intensity are computed from them.

Derived columns: avg_farm_size_ac, share_cultivated, share_farms_under_5ac, share_area_25ac_plus, share_farms_owner, share_farms_tenant, cropping_intensity_pct
