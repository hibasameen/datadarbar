Read before using

One district, one company: districts with a known minority served by another company name it in split_with. Five rows are medium confidence (Naushahro Feroze, Dadu, Shaheed Benazirabad, Lasbela, Upper Chitral). AJK and GB are AJK-ED and GB-WPD, outside NEPRA’s tables. Do not paint a company’s figure onto its districts as if each district had it.
