Definitions and limitations

Values only — the words belong to place_indicator_index, because repeating a label 90,000 times is most of what makes a payload large. map_key names the PBS 2023 shape(s) a row is drawn on and follows the same convention as census_unit_map: a unit that was later split names all of its successors, so count units and not shapes when totalling. 73 of the fields here are provenance rather than indicators — dhs_coverage, hies_inherited_from, the *_n_obs and *_low_n flags — which is why they have no index entry.

Pakistan district and tehsil indicators on 2023 boundaries — data dictionary
FieldTypeDefinition
levelVARCHARdistrict or tehsil

group_keyVARCHARthe indicator group, joining to place_indicator_index

yearVARCHARthe year, where the series has one; NULL where it does not
valueDOUBLEthe figure — unit depends on the indicator, see the index
