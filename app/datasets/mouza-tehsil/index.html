Definitions and limitations

Every column is a COUNT OF MOUZAS (revenue villages), never of people or households — a mouza of 12,000 and a mouza of 300 each count once. PBS publishes numerators without a denominator, and its own indicator blocks disagree about how many mouzas answered: only 33 of the 544 enumerated tehsils give a single consistent base, and blocks can differ by up to 181. Take each block's own row sum as its base rather than TotalMauzaCount. Existance/NotExistance pairs are exclusive; drinking water, health facility type, fuel, street surface and media are multiple response, so they can sum past the base. The frame is rural — cities are not revenue villages — but mouzas that urbanise stay in it (4.9% urban, 2.4% partly urban). 51 tehsils return all zeros: AJK and GB were not enumerated, nor were Mand and Tump in Kech or Kallag in Panjgur. Join to the ADM3 geography through mouza_crosswalk. Column names keep PBS's own casing and spelling; DuckDB matches them case-insensitively.
