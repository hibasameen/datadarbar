Pakistan open data catalogue

Download documented census, trade, budget, national accounts, poverty and State Bank of Pakistan datasets. Each table has field definitions, source information and limitations.

  • Pakistan federal budget line itemsFederal budget line items from the Budget in Brief documents, FY2009-10 → FY2026-27.
  • Pakistan district census and survey indicatorsEvery district-level indicator in Data Darbar, one row per district × indicator × year.
  • Pakistan statistical source-file catalogueIndex of every source file collected for the warehouse, with its upstream URL.
  • Pakistan large-scale manufacturing indexMonthly Quantum Index of Manufacturing (large-scale manufacturing).
  • Pakistan manufacturing sector indicesLSM indices by manufacturing sector, annual and monthly, with CMI weights.
  • Pakistan Mouza Census geographic crosswalkMaps each PBS tehsil to the ADM3 polygon Data Darbar maps it on.
  • Pakistan rural facilities by tehsil, Mouza Census 2020Mouza Census 2020 facility counts, one row per PBS tehsil.
  • Pakistan district multidimensional poverty estimatesAlkire-Foster multidimensional poverty index by district, from PSLM 2019-20 microdata.
  • Pakistan national accounts and GDP tablesNational accounts / GDP series, 1951-52 → 2025-26 (PBS 2015-16 base).
  • Pakistan monetary and external statistics, SBP seriesThe macro time-series panel: every observation of every SBP series held here.
  • State Bank of Pakistan series catalogueEvery SBP EasyData series held here: name, unit, frequency, coverage, method note.
  • Pakistan tehsil night-time lightsNight-time light radiance by tehsil and year (June VIIRS composites).
  • Pakistan tehsil wealth, population and satellite indicatorsTehsil-level (ADM3) satellite measures: relative wealth, population, night-lights.
  • Pakistan imports and exports by HS8 product and country8-digit HS imports and exports, by commodity and partner country, FY2015-16 → FY2024-25.

The browser query tool opens these same tables. Read the units and warnings before combining or summing rows.
