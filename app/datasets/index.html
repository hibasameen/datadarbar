Before you combine tables

Seven habits the tables need.

Published or derived

Every table says whose figures it holds. As published: the source’s own. Derived: Data Darbar constructed them — computed, estimated from survey microdata, aggregated from satellite grids or points, or modelled — and they are not official statistics. Includes derived: the source’s figures plus columns we built, each tagged derived in its field list.

Join on a code, not a name

Districts join on district_code (PBS 2023) or district_key (the older 147-district slug); census units on dds_id. dd_id is not unique: 591 census units share 471 of them. Which key is safe.

Units are per table

Trade is in thousand rupees, the budget in Rs million, national accounts vary by table, and every State Bank series carries its own unit in sbp_series_catalog. Read the unit before you sum.

Long tables

place_indicators, district_indicators and sbp_observations hold one row per place or series, indicator and period. Filter to one indicator first, then PIVOT.

Fiscal years

Pakistan's fiscal year runs July to June and is written 2024-25. State Bank series are stamped on the last day of the period, so FY2023-24 appears as 2024-06-30.

NULL is not zero

A NULL is suppressed or unpublished. Survey districts with too few respondents are flagged (low_n) and should be left out of rankings.

Two trade bases

trade_hs8 is PBS customs data by product; the State Bank's balance-of-payments series are on a payments basis. They will not reconcile.

Licences and reuse

Data Darbar’s own work — the cleaning, joins, crosswalks and derived columns — is CC BY 4.0. The figures underneath keep their publisher’s terms, and a table is never offered on freer terms than its source allows. Most of the catalogue is open: PBS releases its statistics under an open licence that allows commercial reuse with attribution. The exceptions are marked on each table: State Bank of Pakistan series and Meta’s wealth index are non-commercial, OpenStreetMap rows are share-alike, and several public bodies publish without stating a licence, so cite them and ask before commercial reuse. Each table’s page and its entry in catalog.json name the terms that apply. Terms checked 4 October 2026; this is a guide, not legal advice.

PBS open licenceOpen

PBS Data Dissemination Policy (August 2026), section 9.1: copy, adapt and redistribute, commercially too, acknowledging PBS as the source, marking changes, and not presenting derived figures as official PBS statistics.

For code

The files are static Parquet: query them over HTTP without downloading.

import duckdb
duckdb.sql("INSTALL httpfs; LOAD httpfs;")
duckdb.sql("""SELECT district, pending_end, pending_per_100k
  FROM 'https://darbar.adaad.org/data/warehouse/ljcp_court_districts.parquet'
  WHERE year = 2024 ORDER BY pending_per_100k DESC LIMIT 10""").show()
