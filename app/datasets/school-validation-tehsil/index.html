Definitions and limitations

Locates holes inside districts. 'absent from harvest' here is mostly a NAME mismatch rather than a hole: PBS enumerates sub-tehsils the registers do not carry (88 of Balochistan's 139), and the Karachi sub-divisions do not exist in the SELD roster. Sindh matches 104 of its 110 rural talukas. Read flags with the same caveats as school_validation_district, and with min_m = 5 mouzas rather than 10.
