Read before using

Selected rural districts of Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Sindh, not a national sample, and a different set of districts each round: compare a district with itself only where it was surveyed in both rounds. Rounds 2–3 used FAO’s first questionnaire and rounds 4–6 its second. Round 3 was in the field in March–April 2022, before the floods; round 4 in 2023, after them. Hunger questions ask about the last 30 days. Shares count only households that answered (not don’t-know, refused or not asked) and are withheld where fewer than 30 did. Usta Muhammad’s round 6 sample sits under Jaffarabad, which the 2023 census counts it within.
