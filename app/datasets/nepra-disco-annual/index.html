Read before using

Read straight off NEPRA’s tables, which is why row_label and col_label are carried as printed rather than normalised: the tables change shape between editions and a single schema across nineteen years would have to invent correspondences. value_raw is the text as printed; filter on series, table_no and row_label to pull one measure.
