Read before using

A model (gradient boosting on satellite aerosol, TROPOMI, ERA5 and GEOS-CF) at 0.1°, mean absolute error 14–23 µg/m³ against 2025 ground monitors. Two years: a map, not a trend, and not an independent check on the monitors it was trained against. Population weights are WorldPop 2020.
