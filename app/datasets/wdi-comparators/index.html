Read before using

World Development Indicators for Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Afghanistan, Iran, Egypt, Indonesia, Nigeria and Türkiye, with South Asia, lower-middle income and the world as context: population, growth, income per head, inflation, unemployment, trade, investment, remittances, tax, literacy, life expectancy, fertility, child mortality, poverty and electricity. Several are modelled estimates rather than national figures - unemployment is the ILO’s model (SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS); SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS is the national estimate - and original_source names who produced each. For Pakistan’s own series prefer the PBS and SBP tables; this is for comparison.
