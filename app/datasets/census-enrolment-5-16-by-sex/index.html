Definitions and limitations

Successor districts are summed into their 2017 parents (constituents lists them), so rates are on the same frame as school_access_district; 130 rows. *_in_school_pct = enrolled ÷ population aged 5–16. *_never and *_out_of_school follow PBS's definitions (never attended; never attended plus dropped out). This is the age-matched outcome the piece uses; Table 12's all-ages complement runs about 0.7 points higher.
