Population by census 1951-2023, by district and province

Population at every census from 1951 to 2023, for Pakistan, each province and each district, on boundaries that hold still.

Read before using

One series per place for a chart of population over time. Pakistan and the provinces as PBS prints them (1951-1998, table 1 of the administrative units table). Districts as footprints on the 2023 frame that are the same ground in every year shown: a 1998 district with the 2017 districts it became, joined to its neighbour where the two cannot be told apart - Lahore and Kasur, which exchanged ground between 1998 and 2017, are one series. 1951 and 1961 appear only where PBS counted the footprint on its own that year. 1998 and earlier are PBS’s restatement on 1998 districts; 2017 is table 1 of the 2017 census; 2023 is table 1 of the 2023 census. Each year’s district rows sum to the published national total.
