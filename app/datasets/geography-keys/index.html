Definitions and limitations

Six identifiers name places in this warehouse and they are not interchangeable; unique_per_place is the column that decides whether a join is safe. dd_id is the trap: 591 census sub-districts share 471 of them, so joining on it and keeping one row per polygon returns a plausible answer with units missing. Every count here is measured from the boundary files rather than asserted.
