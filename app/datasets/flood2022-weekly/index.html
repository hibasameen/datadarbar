2022 floods by district and week

Read before using

One product per window, one frame. coverage is the share of the district the satellite saw cloud-free; low_coverage flags weeks under half. 22 source cells were blank and are counted as zero, flagged in flooded_km2_blank_in_source. On COD’s 2022 districts, as flood2022_district.
