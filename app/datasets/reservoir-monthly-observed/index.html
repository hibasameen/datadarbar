Read before using

Months with an observation only. Global Water Watch’s own monthly series carries values into unobserved months and disagrees with the observations in 1,074 reservoir-months; gww_disagrees_20pct flags them. Surface area, not storage. Validated against Tarbela’s hydel generation, r of about 0.6.
