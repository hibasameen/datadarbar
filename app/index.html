What this is careful about

Both censuses are drawn on the boundaries PBS published for 2023, so the same place means the same place in each year. Where a district split, the unit map records it rather than silently splitting a number. Rates are recomputed from summed numerators and denominators, never averaged. An absent figure is marked absent, not drawn as a zero.
