Pakistan in Numbers

Explore Pakistan's census, survey,
trade and macroeconomic data

Data Darbar turns Pakistan's official statistics — the census and household surveys, 8-digit trade, national accounts, the federal budget and eight decades of monetary data — into interactive maps, Atlas-style treemaps and time series, from the rupee in 1947 down to poverty in every tehsil. Pick a view to begin.

District & Tehsil Map
Explore Pakistan district by district, and village by village
Census, PSLM, labour force and household data mapped across all 141 districts, and the Mouza Census across 553 tehsils — with a 2017 vs 2023 comparison.
Open →
Trade Atlas
What Pakistan buys and sells
Every 8-digit product as a treemap, grouped by sector. Drill from textiles down to individual commodities, and see top partners — 2015–2024.
Open →
GDP & Budget
Output by sector, and the federal budget
GDP growth and the economy by sector and its input-output flows, plus the federal budget — receipts and spending broken down as a treemap.
Open →
Monetary & External
Inflation, interest rates, the rupee, remittances and the banks
The exchange rate since 1947 and the policy rate since 1956, with inflation, the interbank curve, foreign exchange reserves, the current account — with every inflow and outflow broken down — and where remittances come from.
Open →
Poverty & Wealth
Deprivation, wealth and activity from space
A multidimensional poverty index built from household microdata, plus satellite measures of relative wealth, population and night-time lights — down to all 554 tehsils.
Open →
Query the Data
For analysts — SQL, straight in the browser
Every table behind Data Darbar — districts, poverty, 8-digit trade, national accounts, the budget and 1,300 State Bank series — as one warehouse. Full SQL in the browser, no signup, nothing sent to a server; export CSV or read the same files from Python and R.
