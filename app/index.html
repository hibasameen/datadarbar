About the data

Data Darbar is an open explorer of Pakistan's official statistics, drawn from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the Finance Division. Every figure is traceable to its published source, and the underlying cleaned data is available as a queryable database.

Census & surveys2017 & 2023 Population Census, PSLM, Labour Force Survey, HIES — district level.
External trade8-digit HS imports & exports by commodity and country, 2015–2024.
National accountsGDP by sector, growth and expenditure, 1951–2026 (2015-16 base).
Federal budgetReceipts & expenditure from the "Budget in Brief", 2009–2027.
Poverty & satelliteAlkire–Foster MPI from PSLM microdata; Meta Relative Wealth Index, WorldPop and VIIRS night-lights at tehsil level.
