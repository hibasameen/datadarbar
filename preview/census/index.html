Sources & method

Counts come from the frozen, verified Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census release. Each area combines whole source units so the same statistical area can be followed in both years. Rates use summed counts and their own denominators.

What the boundaries mean

These are comparison areas, not a list of current administrative districts. Jhang + Toba Tek Singh and Kachhi + Nasirabad each appear once. Their individual historical allocations remain uncertain.

The existing map file supplies illustrative outlines. Combined outlines are dissolved and invalid shapes repaired for display only. The six areas with former frontier regions remain in all totals and downloads. The map file does not separately identify those regions, so their inclusion in the displayed outlines cannot be verified.

AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan and administrative units created after the census are outside this release.
